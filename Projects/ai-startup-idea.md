# AI Startup Idea

## Startup Name
StudyFlow AI

---

## Problem
Students struggle with:
- time management
- revision planning
- productivity
- organizing study material

---

## Solution
An AI-powered study assistant that helps students:
- create study schedules
- summarize notes
- generate quizzes
- improve productivity

---

## Features
- AI timetable generator
- Revision planner
- AI note summarizer
- Quiz generator
- Productivity tracker

---

## Target Audience
- School students
- IBDP students
- Competitive exam students

---

## Business Model
Freemium subscription model:
- Free basic features
- Paid premium productivity tools

---

## Future Expansion
- Mobile app
- AI mentor chatbot
- Collaboration tools for students

---

## Skills Used
- AI concepts
- Prompt engineering
- Business thinking
- Problem solving
